## 🎯 What Is This?

The **Maven Systems Bookkeeper Rate Calculator** is a professional pricing tool designed exclusively for bookkeepers. It eliminates the guesswork from service pricing by automatically calculating every rate the moment you enter your hourly rate — no spreadsheets, no formulas, no errors.

---

## ❓ What Problem Does It Solve?

Most bookkeepers price their services inconsistently. They either:
- Undercharge because they're unsure of market rates
- Spend hours manually updating Excel spreadsheets
- Send clients quotes with calculation mistakes

This tool solves all three problems in one place.

- 💰 **50+ services covered** — Accounting, Reconciliations, Payroll, Compliance, Ancillary
