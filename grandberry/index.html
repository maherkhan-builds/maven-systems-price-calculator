How it works

Three small habits. One closer family.

No feeds. No scores. Just a few warm moments a day, with big buttons and plain words.

Morning check-in

Six short questions. Sleep, mood, comfort, water, breakfast, usual medicine. One tap each, no typing.

Rose reviews every answer before anyone sees it.

Family Corner

Short notes and voice messages from the family. Rose replies with one big, clearly labelled record button.

She sees the exact names of who will hear her reply.

Memory Moments

A gentle prompt invites a story. Her voice is saved as a family memory, to keep for the grandkids.

Transcripts are optional drafts she can fix or remove.
Our promises

Connection, not surveillance.

A missed check-in can mean many things. Maybe Rose just went for a walk. Grandberry helps the family reach out kindly, and never raises an alarm.

Grandberry will

  • Keep everything private until its owner chooses to share
  • Show exact names before anything is shared
  • Let older adults edit, delete or take back what they shared
  • Keep working when AI is slow or switched off

Grandberry will never

  • Diagnose, give medical advice or call emergency services
  • Treat a missed check-in as an emergency
  • Sell family data or show ads
  • Use family stories to train general AI models

Why we are building this

Every voice is worth keeping.

Grandberry started with a simple feeling. Everyday contact with ageing parents was slipping away, and so were their stories. We want to help families hold on, gently.
